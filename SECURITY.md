# Security and Privacy

Do not upload real applicant data to this repository.

Never commit:

- Social Security numbers,
- dates of birth,
- legal IDs,
- case numbers,
- benefit card numbers,
- immigration documents,
- medical documents,
- disability records,
- bank records,
- shelter locations tied to a person,
- domestic violence or trafficking disclosures,
- signatures.

Use placeholders in examples.
