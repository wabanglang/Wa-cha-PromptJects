# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve community social aid, universal access, individual usefulness, privacy, accuracy, or low-cognitive-load completion.

## Good contributions

- clearer prompts,
- shorter worksheets,
- better privacy separation,
- better field maps,
- stronger no-fabrication checks,
- translations,
- accessibility improvements,
- jurisdiction-specific notes with sources,
- printable templates,
- advocate workflow aids.

## Required contribution rules

1. Do not add fake eligibility instructions.
2. Do not encourage lying on official forms.
3. Do not collect sensitive applicant data in examples.
4. Do not include real Social Security numbers, DOBs, case numbers, medical records, bank records, or addresses.
5. Mark sensitive fields as private human-only.
6. Preserve applicant review before submission.
7. Keep examples generic.

## Pull request checklist

- [ ] The contribution reduces burden without reducing truthfulness.
- [ ] Sensitive fields are separated.
- [ ] Official-form claims are sourced or marked as assumptions.
- [ ] The prompt does not fabricate identity, income, disability, immigration, legal, medical, or household facts.
- [ ] The output is usable by nontechnical people.
